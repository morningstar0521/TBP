DROP YOUR PHOTOS HERE
=====================

Name them exactly like this:

  1.jpeg  2.jpeg  3.jpeg  4.jpeg
  5.jpeg  6.jpeg  7.jpeg  8.jpeg   -> the spinning 3D gallery

  hero.jpeg     -> polaroid on the first screen
  letter.jpeg   -> polaroid next to the letter
  t1.jpeg       -> timeline: "the early era"
  t2.jpeg       -> timeline: "the war years"
  t3.jpeg       -> timeline: "now"

Then just refresh index.html.
Any photo you skip shows an empty slot - nothing breaks.

Using .png instead of .jpeg? Open index.html and
search for ".jpeg" then replace with ".png".

Best sizes: gallery photos look best portrait (3:4).
Timeline photos look best landscape (4:3).
